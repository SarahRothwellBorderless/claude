What is a supply chain legal register?

A legal register lists the laws and regulations that apply to your business and supply chain, so you can track obligations and evidence compliance.

How is human rights due diligence important?

It helps you identify, prevent and address the human rights impacts linked to your operations and suppliers, and is increasingly a legal requirement.
