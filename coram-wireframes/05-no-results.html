Notes for this screen

Workshop decision agreed at the 24 Nov workshop or in the brief Client copy wording taken from the November content Guess our assumption, to check Open question still to be decided

  1. 1Workshop decisionA no-match page must never suggest records don't exist. 'Faladon' is the spelling from a real request email. The directory lists it as Fallodon. The heading wording is ours.
  2. 2GuessA spelling suggestion ('did you mean'). This needs fuzzy matching in the search engine (a question for the build). When there's truly nothing close, this block is left out and the page starts with 'Try another search'.
  3. 3Workshop decisionPractical ways to search again come before anything else, so the person can keep going.
  4. 4GuessFallback to the councils or archives that covered the place. These are always labelled 'possible', never 'confirmed'. This needs place-to-council data, which may not exist for every entry.
  5. 5Workshop decisionExplains openly why the data has gaps, using reasons from the workshop: organisations that didn't respond to the FOI requests, courts coming later, and name changes. 'Say what it will do and do what it says.'
  6. 6Client copy'You do not need to know exactly which records exist before making enquiries' is from 'Where to start'. The care and adoption links are kept separate.
  7. 7GuessBirth certificate prompt from the workshop. Most useful in the no-match case, where a different place name can unlock the search.
  8. G1Workshop decisionQuick exit and support on every page. Desktop: header button plus a side tab. Mobile: a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen. Quick exit should replace the page and clear it from history. The site tree note asks for 'Exit, support and pins on sidebar'. We've read 'pins' as map pins, which needs checking.
