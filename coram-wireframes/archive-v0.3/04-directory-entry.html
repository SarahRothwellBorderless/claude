Before you get in touch6

I was adoptedI was in careI'm a relative

Contact the organisation and ask how it wants to receive your request. It helps to give:

  • your full name and any previous names
  • your date of birth
  • the places and dates you remember, even if they're rough
  • proof of identity, if they ask for it. For adoption, this is often a birth certificate.
