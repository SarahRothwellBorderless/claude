Before you search

  • The directory lists places and organisations, not people. You can't search for a person's records here.5
  • An entry helps you identify who to contact, but does not confirm that your individual records exist.
  • The directory is not complete. Not every organisation has sent us its details yet.
  • If you have your birth certificate, look at the place of birth and your birth mother's address. Those places are good things to search for.7
